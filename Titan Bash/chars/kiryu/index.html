Kiryu

"Kiryu, you understand what I say."

Archetype: Pressurer

Difficulty: [*****]

Designer Notes

My idea for Kiryu is a machine with a single purpose, to defeat the enemy. In the case of the movies it was a single target being Godzilla. I want him to be aggressive to bring over that relentless feel to Titan Bash, and I think the lock on mechanic helps solidify that pretty well!

Do note this Kiryu will be somewhat special, his weapons won't be out and about for display, they'll be inside his metal body until a move using them is activated, in which they will pop out. I am doing this just to make the start-ups more audible and in turn fairer.

Base Moveset

[Special] Lock-On

Special is a lock on mechanic. Attacking opponent that is locked on alters certain moves.

Notes

This doesn't actually lock onto opponents to aim for you, just the visual effect.

Considering: Lock-On also makes attacks have higher damage. Not too sure about this due to balance

[G] Iron Cyclone

Kiryu throws both arms forward, grabbing any opponent caught in his path. With them firmly in his grasp, he spins to build momentum before hurling them away.

Notes

Properties: Combo Ender, Grab, Knockaway

[A1] MASER Edge

Kiryu lunges forward, briefly activating his jet boosters to propel himself ahead. As he advances, he deploys his wrist blade and drives it into the first opponent he reaches. He then channels electricity through the blade, overloading and electrocuting the opponent.

Notes

Properties: Combo Starter/Extender, Dash Attack, Stun

Main way of getting close. It has high endlag as to not encourage throwing it out in neutral as much.

[A2] Rockets Away!

Kiryu deploys a pair of rocket cannons from atop his shoulders and unleashes a relentless bombardment of rockets directly ahead.

Notes

Properties: Neutral, Projectile

This is not meant to combo start nor end, it leaves both parties in neutral (if the last couple of shots hit).

Considering: attacking locked on opponents also gives slightly extra damage

[A3] MASER Cannon

Kiryu opens his jaws as the emitter housed within his mouth generates and amplifies concentrated microwave energy. Once fully charged, it releases the gathered energy as a single powerful Maser blast, rapidly heating and scorching anybody caught in its path.

Kiryu's beam is unable to extend in combo.

With lock on it becomes Absolute Zero Cannon

Notes

Properties: Neutral, Beam

[A3] Absolute Zero Cannon

Kiryu unfolds the panels of his chest, revealing a large cannon bore. Energy gathers within the exposed weapon and forms into an orb, which is rapidly cooled down to -273.15°C. Kiryu then fires the orb forward, flash-freezing anything caught in its path. Any attack to a frozen opponent shatters the ice, which adds damage on top, or after a certain time happens by itself.

The freeze itself stuns, but when shattered the opponent is out of stun, requiring an attack like A1/UA2 to combo start.

Notes

Properties: Projectile, Stun

The projectile acts like a particle(?). It should dissipate as it moves further, which is why the ultimate helps with the mobility to get close.

Mode Moveset

[Ultimate] Booster Overdrive

Kiryu enters mode; Jet Boosters are deployed into "overdrive" mode, causing a blue coloration, and giving different variants to A1 and A2. The mode is on a fast timer due to how strong it is.

Notes

Properties: Ultimate, I-Frames

[A1] Boost Drive

Kiryu uses the tremendous speed generated by his boosters to surge forward, seemingly vanishing before instantly reappearing ahead. Any opponent caught in his path is struck by a powerful shoulder bash and sent flying.

Notes

Properties: Combo Ender, Dash Attack, Knockaway

Really strong and better than A1 in base. Endlag however is MORE than A1 in base is if whiffed.

[A2] Boost Salvo

Kiryu deploys the launchers mounted on his back and fires a barrage of missiles forward. The missiles explode upon impact, stunning any opponent caught in the blasts. These missiles can only combo start, the startup makes them unable to extend.

With lock on, the missiles gains seeking properties.

Notes

Properties: Combo Starter, Projectile, Seeking

In both versions, Kiryu is positive, meaning he can walk alongside the missiles and cause great pressure to the opponent. Mainly used to better land Absolute Zero Cannon.
