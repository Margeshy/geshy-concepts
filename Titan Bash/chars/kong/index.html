AXE-BOUND KONG

“Monkey see, monkey do.”

Tier: T4
HP: 1150
Archetype: Rushdown/Footsie
Difficulty: [★★★☆☆]

CORE SYSTEM

The general idea is that Kong is smart in battle. He relies on his environment by clinging onto surfaces for mobility, or taking hold of elements (trees, buildings, etc.). In base, he favors mobility. With his axe equipped, he opts for damage and building ultimate.

DESIGNER NOTES

For the moveset, I wanted to introduce two mechanics:
Pushblock pushes back the opponent when the attack is blocked. An alternative over higher blockstam damage.
Environment usage for traversal or buffing his offense.
And in-combo versions so attacks aren't wasteful.

In axe mode, charges are only consumed by ult.

His run speed should be 1-2 values lower than Zilla.

BASE MOVESET

[SPECIAL] King's Axe

Kong reaches over his shoulder, pulls the axe from his back, and immediately surges forward. He leaps toward the opponent and brings the axe down in a heavy slam. The attack applies pushblock.

After the slam, Kong keeps the axe in hand and switches to his axe moveset.

A2, A3, and Ultimate are altered in this state.
A charge meter appears on the left side of the screen and tracks how much beam energy the axe has absorbed. It has two thresholds: 10 charge and 20 charge. Reaching each changes the moveset further.

Once Special comes off cooldown, it can be used again to put the axe away and return to Kong’s base moveset.

NOTES

CD: 30 DMG: 55

Properties: Pushblock, Stance Switch, Utility

At 10 charge, the axe glows faint blue; at 20 charge, it shines brighter.

[G] Jungle's Panoply

Kong reaches out with an open palm and makes a diagonal grab. Against an opponent, Kong catches them, throws them to the ground, and stomps on their torso.

If he grabs an object instead, Kong holds onto it. He can pick up trees, boulders, or buildings, then use the object up to three times in place of his LCs.

On the third use, the object breaks and knocks the opponent away. While Kong is holding an object, he cannot use his other attacks.

NOTES

CD: 23 DMG: 55

Properties: Grab, Knockdown, Utility, Knockback [3rd Use]

[A1] Earthrake

Kong plunges his left arm into the ground and rakes up a spray of soil, hurling it at his opponent. The dirt bursts across them and leaves them stunned.

NOTES

CD: 17 DMG: 35

Properties: Projectile, Combo Extender

Ok so since he uses his left arm here his axe would be in his right hand, which is why it isnt altered like a2/a3.

[A2] Manhattan Stampede

Kong drops onto all fours and charges straight at his opponent, quickly closing the distance. He can recast into A2 or cancel into another attack.

On recast, he rises into a heavy elbow strike, critically stunning the opponent for a combo.
While in-combo, he immediately initiates the elbow strike to knock them away.

NOTES

CD: 20 DMG: 45

Properties: Mobility, Recast, Combo Starter/Knockback

Doing something new with "in-combo" mechanic, just to not let any attacks be wasteful.

[A3] Empire Drop

Kong leaps forward and brings both arms down in an overhead slam. If he misses, he keeps moving with a forward roll that can be steered to reposition. The roll has end lag, leaving him vulnerable to ranged attacks.
Can be used immediately after a normal third M1 to initiate the leaping slam and successfully hit the opponent again.

If used near a wall or building, Kong clings onto the surface. Recasting A3 makes him leap off and slam down again; this follow-up always breaks the opponent's block.

NOTES

CD: 25 DMG: 70

Properties: Mobility, Recast, Block Breaker

The block-breaking follow-up is situational and rewards skilled setup near a surface, its more balanced than something like miregoji's a1+a2 in my opinion.

[ULTIMATE] Dominance Show

Kong pounds his chest to assert dominance, then leaps forward and throws an unguardable grab at the opponent. Holding them in his grip, he spins faster and faster to build momentum before finishing with a powerful throw.

While spinning, Kong can aim the throw and move slowly; nearby enemies are repeatedly damaged.

NOTES

ULT: 80 DMG: 150

Properties: Ultimate, Grab, AOE, Block Bypass

AXE MOVESET

[G] Ragnarok

Kong buries his axe in the opponent’s shoulder, hacking deeply, and releases his grip. He follows with a powerful blow to their torso, crumpling them as he retrieves his axe.

NOTES

CD: 24 DMG: 75

Properties: Grab

[A2] Warpath Pursuit

Kong hurls his axe forward, lodging it in the opponent. He immediately sprints after them and lands a forceful dropkick, driving them backward. As he follows through, Kong pulls the axe free and retrieves it.

At 20 charge, Kong sweeps the opponent’s legs as he retrieves the axe, knocking them off balance and starting a combo.

NOTES

CD: 23/25 DMG: 80/60

Properties: Projectile, Knockback/Combo Starter [20 Charge]

On a successful axe hit, the enemy is stunned until the follow-up is complete.

[A3] Maelstrom Rive

Kong whirls his axe through a series of rapid hits, carries the motion into a full spin, and finishes with a powerful cleave that knocks the opponent back. The axe can absorb beams to build charge, with each beam tick granting 1 charge.

This is Kong’s only way to generate axe charge within the moveset.

NOTES

CD: 26 DMG: 64

Properties: Multi-Hit, Knockback, Utility

The main show for his axe mode! Decided to give it the highest CD out of mode so that charges 10 and 20 take more time between them.

[ULTIMATE] Shining Requiem

At 10 charge, Kong holds his axe aloft and brings it down toward the opponent’s torso. The axe charges are consumed to release a small explosion, harming the opponent and anybody nearby.

At 20 charge, Kong raises the axe, shining brighter than ever, then brings it down toward the opponent’s torso, slashes upward at their arm, cleaves across their chest, and sweeps their leg. With the opponent sprawled on the ground, he delivers a final strike to their neck. The axe charges are consumed to release a giant explosion, brutally damaging all nearby enemies.

NOTES

ULT: 100/135 DMG: 150/250 + 50/150 based on 10/20 axe charge

Properties: Ultimate, AOE
